# Risk Assessment

## Risk ID
RSK-002

## Risk Statement

Employees may physically record passwords on sticky notes, desks, or underneath keyboards due to password fatigue. This creates a risk of unauthorized physical credential theft and can bypass otherwise effective logical access controls.

## Risk Rating

| Measure | Score |
|---|---:|
| Inherent Likelihood | 4/5 |
| Inherent Impact | 3/5 |
| Inherent Risk | 12/25 |
| Residual Likelihood | 1/5 |
| Residual Impact | 3/5 |
| Residual Risk | 3/25 |

## Risk Treatment

**Treatment:** Mitigate

Primary treatment actions:

1. Deploy an Enterprise Password Manager.
2. Introduce passwordless authentication where feasible.
3. Conduct recurring clean-desk and clear-screen inspections.
4. Reinforce security awareness and accountability.

## Expected Outcome

Reduce password exposure caused by insecure physical storage while improving authentication hygiene and control effectiveness.
